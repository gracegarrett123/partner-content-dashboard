Base: the trailing 30-day sponsored content extract (Jul 26–Aug 25, 122 stories, 13 brands, 58,854 PV) — the trailing-7-day pull is too thin most weeks (5 stories this cycle) to read on its own, so it's used only as a sample-size caveat, not a trend.         💡 Key Insights

        Modern Healthcare leads on scale, Automotive News leads on engagement — MH: 25,050 PV (43% of the 30-day sponsored total, 23 stories) at a 1:21 avg dwell, roughly in line with the 1:23 portfolio average; Automotive News: 13,872 PV (24%) at a 1:56 avg dwell, ~40% above portfolio. Together the two brands carry 66% of sponsored volume. Move: lead MH pitches on reach/frequency, lead Auto News pitches on engagement depth. Tableau

        Two formats do most of the work — Publishing Partners placements drove 23,135 PV (39% of the 30-day total) and the Healthcare Insider Podcast added another 7,702 (13%); together just over half of sponsored volume sits in two content formats. Move: prioritize Publishing Partners and podcast-adjacent slots when pitching new sponsored inventory — they're the proven formats. Tableau

        Plastics News is small but sticky — only 1,883 PV across 3 stories, but a 4:37 average dwell, more than 3x the 1:23 portfolio average. Move: package Plastics News as a premium, high-attention niche buy rather than a reach play. Tableau

        Concentration is the structural risk — MH + Auto News = 66% of the 30-day sponsored total; the other 11 brands split the remaining 34%, with Rubber News, Crain's New York and Crain's Grand Rapids each under 2% individually. Move: build a diversified bundle across the thinner brands to de-risk the dependence on two titles. Tableau

        The trailing 7 days is a light week, not a trend — only 5 sponsored stories across 3 brands (313 PV) ran Aug 18–24, versus 122 stories / 13 brands over the trailing 30. Move: don't read week-over-week off this count; track pacing against the 30-day base until weekly volume normalizes. Tableau

        ⚠️ Watch Areas

        Measurement debt: most arrivals are unattributed. "Unknown" is 69% of the 30-day sponsored visit volume (35,939 of 52,250) versus just 6% from Search — nearly 12x more traffic with no captured referrer. Move: fix UTM/referrer tagging; avoid channel-attribution claims in client decks until resolved. Tableau

        Dwell isn't captured on the two biggest single-story wins. The #1 story of the window (Auto News / Reynolds & Reynolds cybersecurity read, 2,929 PV) and Hart Energy's only sponsored story (1,874 PV) both show no dwell data. Move: verify tagging on these placements before citing them as engagement wins in a sponsor recap. Tableau

        Volume of stories isn't converting to attention for Cleveland. Crain's Cleveland ran the most stories in the window (25) for only 2,273 PV and a 0:57 average dwell — the lowest dwell of any brand with double-digit story count. Move: audit placement/format on Cleveland's sponsored slate. Tableau

        Dwell coverage gap portfolio-wide. Only 90 of the 122 sponsored stories in the 30-day window (74%) carry usable dwell data; the other 32 (26%) can't be scored on engagement at all. Move: treat dwell comparisons as directional until tagging coverage improves. Tableau

        Rubber News is effectively inactive. 2 stories, 4 total pageviews in the 30-day window — not enough volume to report on or sell against. Move: don't include Rubber News in current sponsored-performance conversations with clients; flag if new placements are expected. Tableau

        📖 Metrics & caveats Tableau

        Visits / Page Views / Unique Visitors — from the Tableau sponsored content extract (windowed per pull). Unique = distinct users in the window; page views exclude same-session repeats.

        Avg time on page — depth-of-engagement proxy. It is not listen/read time and (for podcasts) not download-based; individual listen data isn't available across platforms.

        Benchmark / pacing — each insight is graded against the 30-day sponsored portfolio average (recomputed every refresh from the live extract) rather than a fixed target, since the underlying pull changes week to week.

        Not yet measured — audience firmographics (company size, industry, named accounts), intent (Bombora), traffic-source mix, and podcast downloads (Libsyn). These require new sources and are the highest-value add for client-grade insights.
